6 · Gatekeeper blocks

When they say…

"He's not available"→ Ask the best time, call back exactly then
"Send an email"→ Get name + direct email, set follow-up date
"What's it about?"→ "Tenders and a two-way referral opportunity"

Never hang up empty-handed: get a name, time, email or follow-up date.

7 · The pitch

The words that book the call

"We have clients who trust us for referrals. Send us any projects or clients looking for a main contractor, and we'll do the same for you. Can we do a quick 15-minute intro call?"

Then offer two specific times. Send the invite straight away.

8 · Do & don't

Rules of the road

DoBe brief, friendly, log every call
Don'tPromise prices, timelines or guaranteed work
DoAsk for tender packs and deadlines
Don'tBadmouth other contractors
